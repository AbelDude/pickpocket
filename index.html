How to Play

Wait until a pedestrian’s orange rectangle is fully inside your blue rectangle. The arrow appears when it’s in place. Press the matching arrow key to pickpocket them and earn cash.

Miss a pickpocket or let someone pass and you lose a heart. Pedestrians get faster for every $50 you steal.
